What HR Express Service Is

  • Immediate, professional HR advice and practical guidance for specific situations.
  • Direct access to experienced HR professionals (organizational developers, employee relations specialists).
  • Flexible, on-demand support on a pay-as-you-go basis (charged flat rate of $100 per hour).
  • A confidential and objective sounding board to discuss employee concerns and workplace challenges.

What HR Express Service is NOT

  • Legal advice, legal interpretations, or active representation in formal employment litigation.
  • Ongoing, full-service HR outsourcing or management of your day-to-day HR administration.
  • A replacement for qualified employment legal counsel when resolving complex statutory disputes.
  • Long-term fractional HR consulting or dedicated, hands-on administrative HR support.
