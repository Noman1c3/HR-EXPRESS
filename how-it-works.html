The Process

How It Works

Choose a time that works best for your schedule using our simple online booking system. If your situation is urgent, give us a call directly, and we will do our best to assist you as quickly as possible.

No long-term contracts. No complicated onboarding process. Just professional HR support when you need it.

Securely pay for your session online. We charge a flat rate of $100 per hour, with no hidden fees, ongoing retainers, or subscription commitments.

Once paid, your session is confirmed, and you will receive call-in details and an automatic calendar invite.

Connect with one of our experienced HR professionals via phone. We'll listen carefully to your situation, ask clarifying questions, and provide clear, actionable guidance.

After the call, you will get a summary of what was discussed, recommended action items, and any relevant templates or resources to guide you.

Our Standards

What to Expect

Listen Carefully

We listen closely to the details of your situation to understand the core workplace or employee challenges you face.

Ask Clarifying Questions

We ask the right questions to surface key details and check for compliance factors you might not have considered.

Provide Practical HR Guidance

We deliver clear, actionable strategies and real-world advice rather than reading generic manuals or legal statutes.

Help You Understand Next Steps

Explain Recommendations

We explain the reasoning behind our advice, drawing on decades of professional human resources experience.

Every business situation is different. Some clients need a quick answer to an HR question, while others need support thinking through a more sensitive employee matter. Our approach is supportive, professional, and solutions-focused.
