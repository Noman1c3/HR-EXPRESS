Our Founder

Dr. Cindy Cushman, PhD

Cindy is a seasoned Human Resources professional with extensive experience supporting organizations of all sizes, including Harvard University and the Episcopal Diocese of Massachusetts. With a strong background in payroll, organizational development, and conflict resolution, she is passionate about helping businesses create stronger workplaces through practical, people-focused HR solutions.

As the founder of HR2fit, Cindy develops high-quality, customized human resources solutions for small and mid-sized businesses throughout the United States. Through HR Express Service, she helps connect businesses with qualified, experienced HR support services that give business owners peace of mind.

Cindy holds a Bachelor's Degree in Human Resource Management, a Master's Degree in Organizational Development from Bridgewater State University, and her PhD in Conflict Resolution from NOVA Southeastern University.
